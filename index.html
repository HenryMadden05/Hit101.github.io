Composing a Hit: What Really Makes a Song Successful?

Impact of Explicitness and Genre on Track Success

Historically, music success was measured through simple metrics such as chart rankings, sales, and radio plays. Today, streaming has transformed the industry - songs are more accessible to a broader global audience, and success has become dramatically more complex. In this project, we aim to answer: "What really makes a song successful?"

We will explore Spotify track-level data from 2009 to 2025, analyzing features such as track popularity, duration, explicit content, artist popularity, follower count, and genres. To make comparisons meaningful, we will define "popularity" carefully - using Spotify's native 0-100 track-level score, and where appropriate, converting it into categorical tiers (e.g., four quantile-based groupings based on artist followers) to enable cleaner comparisons across visualizations.

This topic matters for artists seeking to understand the patterns behind successful releases, for analysts studying how streaming platforms shape music trends, and for our group to examine whether our personal listening habits reflect popularity patterns.
