Sources & References

Reference 1: Spotify at 15 - How Streaming Reinvented the Music Industry

Source: Variety, 2021

This article examines how Spotify and other streaming giants (Apple Music, YouTube Music) have fundamentally reshaped music industry consumption. Spotify grew from holding roughly 7% of the U.S. music market in 2010 to 83% by the end of 2020. The introduction of free-tier streaming nearly eradicated music piracy and dramatically lowered the cost of genre exploration - listeners no longer needed to buy an album in an unfamiliar genre to try it. This context is directly relevant to our project, as these structural shifts are inseparable from how popularity metrics function today.

Reference 2: Beyond Profits - The Music Industry's Cultural and Financial Impact in 2025

Source: Spotify Newsroom, March 2025

This Spotify newsroom piece discusses streaming's cultural and financial footprint as of 2025, including over $10 billion paid out to artists in royalties in 2024. It also highlights that more than half of artists earning over $1,000 in streaming royalties made the majority of those earnings from listeners outside their home country - underscoring how global accessibility has redefined what "success" means for an artist. This supports our framing of popularity as a multi-dimensional, globally influenced metric.
